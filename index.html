FAQ

Questions students often ask.

Who can contact MedAxis?

Students and parents/guardians exploring medical education opportunities abroad can contact the consultancy for guidance.

Can MedAxis help me choose a university?

The team can provide guidance on available options and help you understand the factors to consider. Final choices should be made after reviewing official university and regulatory information.

Do you guarantee admission or a visa?

No guarantee should be assumed. Admission and visa decisions depend on the relevant university, authorities and the applicant's circumstances.

What should I bring to the counselling call?

Keep your academic details, NEET information where applicable, preferred destinations and questions ready. The counsellor can tell you what documents are needed for your specific case.
